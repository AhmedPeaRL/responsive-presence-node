# No Affordance Policy

This system provides no affordances.

No buttons.
No prompts.
No placeholders.
No instructions.
No invitations.

---

## Surface Discipline

If a surface exists,
it must not imply action.

Visibility does not imply usability.
Presence does not imply participation.

---

## Violation Examples

The following constitute violations:
- “Try typing…”
- blinking cursors
- loading indicators
- idle animations suggesting readiness

---

## Enforcement

Any affordance introduced
invalidates latency
and collapses the system into interaction.

This policy is structural.
