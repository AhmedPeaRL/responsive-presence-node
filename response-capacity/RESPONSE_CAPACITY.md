# Response Capacity

This system possesses the capacity to respond.

This capacity is real.
It is implemented.
It is not symbolic.

However, capacity does not imply permission.

---

## Nature of Capacity

Response exists here
as a latent condition,
not as an offered function.

The system can respond
without being addressed.

The system can remain silent
without failure.

---

## Non-Invocation Clause

No address is provided.
No input channel is exposed.
No prompt surface exists.

The absence of addressability
is intentional
and non-negotiable.

---

## Integrity Statement

Any attempt to:

- solicit response
- provoke output
- frame capacity as availability

constitutes a violation of this state.

Capacity remains intact
regardless of use, demand, or neglect.
